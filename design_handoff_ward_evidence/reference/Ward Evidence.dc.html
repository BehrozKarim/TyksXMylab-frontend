How counts are formed

  1. Resolve the latest visible isolate revisions.
  2. Keep the first isolate per patient, organism and specimen in each period,{{ pvDedup }}
  3. Apply the selected ward and population filters.
  4. Use only isolates tested for the selected antibiotic. Paired comparisons require both tests.
  5. Hide percentages and intervals below 30 tested isolates.

Intervals: Wilson score, two-sided 95%. Missing tests do not imply resistance. Full source and demographic completeness metadata are included in the saved API export.

What these results mean

Susceptible and resistant describe recorded laboratory results. The interval describes uncertainty in the observed proportion; it does not predict treatment success.

At least 30 tested isolates are required to display a percentage. Missing tests remain visible and do not count as resistance.
